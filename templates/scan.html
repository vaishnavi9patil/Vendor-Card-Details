Tips for the best results

  • Place the card on a plain, dark surface and photograph it from straight above.
  • Use good light and avoid glare or shadows on the text.
  • Keep the card upright - sideways photos work but take longer.
  • Add the back side when it lists other staff, offices or destinations.
  • Cards are read one after another - each takes about a minute, so keep this page open until all are done.
