How the sync works

  • App → sheet: new vendors (scanned, added, imported) are added as new rows; vendors edited in the app update their row.
  • Sheet → app: rows typed into the sheet are added to the app - or, if the vendor already exists (same email, same company and contact, or same phone and contact), merged into it. Edits to rows are copied into the app.
  • The App ID column links each row to its vendor - please don't change it. Rows that repeat a vendor are marked "Duplicate of #…".
  • If the same vendor was changed in both places, the app's version is kept.
  • Deleting is never copied: a row deleted in the sheet or a vendor deleted in the app is only reported here.
